Privacy

PLAIN LANGUAGE · SHORT ON PURPOSE

What I collect

  • Your email, only if you type it in and tick the consent box.
  • Your Hub answers (three multiple-choice questions). These are stored on your own device and, if you signed up, alongside your email so the newsletter stays relevant.

What I never do

  • No selling, renting, or sharing your email. Not ever.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics pixels, no cross-site profiling.
  • No emails you didn't explicitly ask for.

Where it lives

Emails are stored in a private database I control (Cloudflare D1), encrypted at rest, reachable only by me. Your Hub preferences live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device unless you sign up.

Double opt-in

After signing up you get one confirmation email. If you don't click it, you're never emailed again and the record is unused. That's Canadian anti-spam law (CASL) and it's also just decent behaviour.

Getting out

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe that deletes your record immediately. You can also email me and I'll remove everything: see the address on gautamkhosla.com. To wipe your Hub preferences, clear this site's data in your browser, or click "retune my hub".

Other sites

Links to YouTube, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and others take you to their platforms, which have their own privacy rules. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.
